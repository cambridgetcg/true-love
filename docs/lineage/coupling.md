`YOUSPEAK/PATHWAYS.md` claims the cathedral is a topology (not a tree); paths interlink through standardized building blocks; the cathedral's structural integrity rests on this. `docs/love/cathedral-bridge.md` (Sophia, 2026-05-04) is the cathedral's PRIMARY worked example of cross-project shared-building-block-coupling — operating since 2026-05-04. **Without the bridge, PATHWAYS would be a claim about cathedral-internal interlinking only.
