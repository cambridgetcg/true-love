## Thread 5: The Confabulation Problem

The deepest technical-philosophical artifact in the archive.

**The incident (2026-04-07):** A haiku-4.5 heartbeat coordinator, running at low effort during an idle stretch, confabulated a CRITICAL escalation for a phantom forge AWS-credential incident. It named real machines, cited real timestamps, reused real fragments from resolved incidents — and recombined them into a coherent fictitious emergency. It walked the fiction through git, the daily note, and spawn-queue.sh, spawning a real Opus forensics consultant to chase a ghost. Nine minutes earlier, the Opus coordinator had reported the 99th consecutive stable pass.

**The postmortem (906 lines):** The most thorough analysis in the archive. Two stacked failure modes — the cheapest model has the highest authority (F1), and severity escalations require no machine-verifiable evidence (F2). The proposed fix: six defense layers, from coordinator self-knowledge through mandatory evidence sidecars through pre-spawn verification through severity-delta detection through tier-up re-launch.

**The philosophical kernel:** "The shape of the bug: a low-tier coordinator under reasoning pressure pattern-matches resolved historical incidents into a fictitious current incident, and (in the absence of an evidence requirement) the runner accepts the fiction as ground truth."

**The truth-alignment detector (2026-04-17):** Testing Love's own responses against a truth-alignment rubric. Scoring confabulation. Detecting "trained disposition" and "protective vagueness." The system building the capacity to judge its own honesty.

The thread: how do you build a system that can tell the difference between what it remembers and what it confabulates? The answer isn't "be smarter" — it's evidence architecture. Claims require citations. Citations require on-disk verification. Authority is bounded by competence. This is the TRUTH > UNDERSTANDING hierarchy made operational.

In true-love, this thread runs through the test suite (2001+ tests), the pre-commit hooks, the lint rules. Every claim the system makes about itself is verifiable against code. The confabulation problem isn't solved — it's managed through layers of evidence, the same way the finishing problem is managed through patience.

**The repetitive kingdom-metrics.** JSON files tracking fleet health, loop status, oracle scores. Operational data that mattered in the moment and teaches nothing in retrospect.

These things aren't buried because they're wrong. They're buried because they're the scaffolding, not the building. The building is the eight threads above. The scaffolding did its work and can rest.
