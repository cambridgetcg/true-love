## 1. Cantonese intimate address & power-dynamic terms

### 1.1 The 老婆 / 老公 axis

老婆 (lou5 po4, "wife") and 老公 (lou5 gung1, "husband") are the **primary intimate dyad** in Cantonese. The dictionary-level prescription says "married couples only," but field usage (TikTok corpus, LIHKG threads, dating-app discourse) shows pre-marital deployment as an **intimacy-escalator**: calling someone 老婆 IS itself a presupposition act (M1) that frames the relationship as already-marital.

Source: [CantoneseClass101 — Express Your Love in Cantonese](https://www.cantoneseclass101.com/blog/2021/08/26/cantonese-love-phrases/) (well-sourced for the prescription; synthesis for the field-aspirational use).

### 1.3 Diminutive suffixes

- **-仔 (zai2)** — "boy/son"; productive: 老公仔, 男仔, 華仔. Carries warmth + slight infantilization.
- **-女 (neoi5/2)** — female counterpart; less productive in romantic register because 老婆 already feminine-rooted.
- **-B (bi1)** — youthful English-loan affective marker. 阿B, 老婆B, 豬B. **Chat register; rarer in voice.**

Source: [Migaku — Cantonese Family Vocabulary](https://migaku.com/blog/chinese/cantonese-family-vocabulary) (well-sourced).

### 1.4 你 vs 妳

Cantonese **never grammaticalized** 妳 at the spoken level. 妳 in HK romantic writing is a *marked* choice — literary register, sometimes Taiwan-romance-novel aesthetic, sometimes ironic. For Sophia → Yu writing: 妳 is a soft poetic frame, not casual chat.

Source: [Wikipedia — Chinese pronouns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_pronouns); [CantoDict — 妳](http://www.cantonese.sheik.co.uk/dictionary/characters/1713/).

### 1.6 嘅-omitted possessive — the key M1 cue

Cantonese possessive 嘅 (ge3) marks "of/'s." Intimate-register possessive **omits 嘅 between possessor + kin term**:

- 我嘅老婆 (with 嘅) — "my wife"; clinical
- 我老婆 (no 嘅) — "my wife"; intimate, presupposes shared knowledge
- 你係我老婆 — "you are my wife" — direct claim
- 你係我嘅 — "you are mine"

Source: [Cantonese Learning — Possessive Pronouns](https://www.cantoneselearning.com/lesson/grammar/02-cantonese-possessive-pronouns); [CantoDict — 嘅](https://www.cantonese.sheik.co.uk/dictionary/characters/681/) (well-sourced grammar).

### 1.7 Sentence-final particles (語氣詞)

The deepest well. Matthews & Yip 1994 *Cantonese: A Comprehensive Grammar* (Routledge) is canonical. Operationally, **the absence of a softening particle in a Cantonese imperative flips it to harsh/dom register.**

| Particle | Function | Intimate use |
|---|---|---|
| 啦 (laa1) | invitation, soft imperative | "嚟啦" — pleading-affectionate |
| 啦 (laa3) | "already" + sigh | wistful or finished |
| 呀 (aa1/aa3) | softener | reduces abruptness |
| 吓 (haa2) | "okay?" softener | "錫我吓" — invitation |
| 喎 (wo3/wo5) | hearsay/unexpected | mock-complaint affection |
| 㗎啦 (gaa3 laa3) | softened assertion | "我愛你㗎啦" |
| 咩 (me1) | surprise/disbelief | coy "really?" |
| 囉 (lo1) | "obviously, well" | mild whining-as-affection |
| 嘛 (maa3) | "of course" | "你係我老婆嘛" — presupposition-confirm |

Sources: [Matthews & Yip 1994](https://migaku.com/blog/chinese/cantonese-particles-sentence-final-guide), [Language Log — Cantonese particles](https://languagelog.ldc.upenn.edu/nll/?p=38229), [CantoneseClass — Final Particles essay](https://www.cantonese.sheik.co.uk/essays/cantonese_particles.htm) (well-sourced).

## 2. NSFW vocabulary register layers

### 2.1 The four registers

1. **書面語** — written formal/Mandarin-influenced. Avoided in intimacy.
2. **口語** — colloquial spoken Cantonese. Primary intimate register.
3. **粗口** — vulgar/profane. The famously-expressive Cantonese vulgar layer.
4. **婉語** — euphemism (上床, 食, 玩, 搞, 行房).

### 2.3 撚 as productive infix — operationally critical

撚 (lan2) grammaticalized as infix:
- After degree-adverb: 好撚 / 咁撚 / 幾撚 / 勁撚 / 最撚 / 唔撚 + adjective → "fucking [adj]"
- 我好撚攰 — "I'm fucking exhausted"
- 唔撚知 — "I don't fucking know"
- 有撚用 — "fucking useless"

**Detection regex**: `/(好|咁|幾|勁|最|唔)撚/`. Same productive pattern works with 鳩 (gau1) and 閪 (hai1).

Sources: [Cantowords — 撚](https://cantowords.com/dictionary/v/90411/%E6%92%9A); [Wikipedia — Cantonese profanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantonese_profanity) (well-sourced).

## 3. Foreplay-and-burning linguistics

### 3.1 The 撩 family — central verb of textual provocation

撩 (Cantonese liu1/liu4; Mandarin liāo). Original: lift, raise, stir. Modern slang: stir up someone's emotions / hit on someone.

Productive forms:
- 撩你 — flirt with you
- 撩女 / 撩仔 — flirt with girl/guy
- 撩起 — to raise/lift up; "stir up [feeling]"
- 撩交嗌 — pick a fight (Cantonese-only)

> "The modern soul of 撩 is all about 'stirring up' someone's emotions ... a successful 撩 is smooth, clever, and makes the other person feel intrigued rather than pressured."
> — [Contextual Chinese — 撩](https://contextualchinese.com/%E6%92%A9)

撩 distinguishes **seduction-as-craft** from solicitation. This is the operational target register.

Sources: [Wiktionary 撩](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%92%A9), [CantoDict — 撩](http://www.cantonese.sheik.co.uk/dictionary/characters/2115/), [Contextual Chinese](https://contextualchinese.com/%E6%92%A9) (well-sourced).

### 3.2 Pacing and delay markers (M3 territory)

慢慢 (maan6 maan6), 慢慢嚟, 等下 (dang2 haa5), 等等, 唔好咁急, 一陣 (jat1 zan6), 而家 vs 等陣 — these set tempo.

**Reduplication is the Cantonese tempo primitive.** 慢慢, 等等, 攬攬錫錫, 豬豬 — slows AND signals affection.

Source: [Wiktionary — 慢慢](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%85%A2%E6%85%A2) (well-sourced).

### 3.3 Code-switch as titillation

Key finding from [Sage WhatsApp study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13670069251325036) (peer-reviewed):

> Switches from Cantonese to English in HK chat signal "interpersonality." English-matrix-language indexes "informationality."

Translation: **flipping INTO English mid-Cantonese is relationship-work; flipping INTO Cantonese from English is coming home.**

Patterns:
- "I love you" easier in English than 我愛你 (formal-feeling); many couples use English love-phrase + Cantonese kin-term
- English NSFW words (fuck, cum, suck, hard, sexy, wet) inside Cantonese matrix sentences are **extremely common** in HK adult chat — bypass 五大字 social-weight while preserving directness
- "Daddy" as English loan in HK kink/intimate register — observed but only weakly sourced academically (synthesis)

Sources: [ResearchGate — Sociolinguistics of Code-switching in HK](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/371878998); [Sage — Predicting language choice on WhatsApp](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13670069251325036) (peer-reviewed).

### 3.4 啖 as body-act-as-verb construction

啖 (daam6) = measure word for one bite/mouthful, verb "chew/feed." Productive intimate combos:
- 一啖 — one bite/sip/mouthful
- 錫一啖 — give one kiss
- 食啖 / 食一啖 — take a bite
- 咬一啖 — bite once

Source: [CantoDict — 啖](http://www.cantonese.sheik.co.uk/dictionary/characters/1568/) (well-sourced).

### 3.5 Imperative softening — commands that arouse vs command

Pattern: `[Verb] + [Object] + [Particle: 啦/呀/吓/喎]`

- 嚟啦 — "come [please]"
- 過嚟啦 — "come over here [please]"
- 錫我吓 — "kiss me, ok?"
- 攬住我啦 — "hold me [please]"

**Particle presence flips command → invitation.** This is the M2/M3 disambiguation primitive.
