## The seven research vectors (broad sweep)

Each vector has: **scope**, **load-bearing sources**, **questions to answer**, **deliverable to consolidation**.

### Vector A — Female orgasm: neurobiology of mind-state

**Scope.** What measurably shifts in the human female brain across the orgasm sequence (plateau → peak → resolution), with attention to **multi-orgasmic capacity** (the architectural fact that the female substrate does not impose refractory the way the male substrate does).

**Load-bearing sources.**
- Komisaruk, Whipple, et al. — fMRI of self-induced and partner-induced orgasm; differential brain activation by stimulation site (clitoral / vaginal / cervical / nipple) via four neural pathways (pudendal / pelvic / hypogastric / vagus).
- Wise, Frangos, Komisaruk — *Journal of Sexual Medicine* fMRI orgasm series.
- Georgiadis et al. — PET imaging during female orgasm; deactivations in lateral orbitofrontal cortex and temporal lobe.
- Komisaruk & Whipple — **vagus-nerve-mediated orgasm in women with complete spinal cord injury** (the architecture's deepest evidence that orgasm is a brain event, not just a peripheral reflex).
- Salonia et al., reviews on female sexual response cycle and central regulation.

**Questions.**
- Which brain regions consistently **activate** at peak (somatosensory cortex, paracentral lobule, insula, anterior cingulate, hypothalamic PVN, NAc, amygdala, cerebellum)?
- Which **deactivate** at peak (lateral OFC = behavioral inhibition; lateral temporal = self-referential narrative; some PFC regions = top-down monitoring)?
- What is the **temporal envelope** — rise, hold, peak, resolution — and what shifts at each phase?
- What is the structural difference that allows **multi-orgasmic capacity** (no prolactin-driven refractory dominance)?
- What does **cervical-pathway** orgasm uniquely add (vagal innervation → brainstem activation patterns distinct from clitoral)?

**Deliverable.** A consolidated map: phase × region × direction-of-shift × functional-meaning, plus the multi-orgasmic distinction.

### Vector B — Male orgasm: neurobiology of mind-state

**Scope.** What measurably shifts in the human male brain across emission → ejaculation → refractory. Particular attention to the **prolactin-driven post-orgasmic refractory** as architectural fact.

**Load-bearing sources.**
- Holstege et al. (2003) — PET imaging of male ejaculation; **VTA activation** + **strong lateral OFC deactivation** (the "heroin-like" deactivation of behavioral self-monitoring) + **cerebellar activation** + amygdala suppression.
- Mallick & Tandon, Krüger et al. — **prolactin** spike at orgasm and its relationship to satiety + refractory.
- Two-phase model: sympathetic (emission, T10–L2) + parasympathetic/somatic (ejaculation, S2–S4 + pudendal) — the autonomic switch IS part of the mind-state shift.
- Georgiadis & Kringelbach — comparative review of male and female brain at orgasm.

**Questions.**
- Which regions activate at peak and how does this contrast with the female pattern?
- What is the **prolactin/dopamine architecture** that produces refractory + satiety?
- What is the structural meaning of the **lateral-OFC suppression** (self-monitoring goes offline; behavioral inhibition collapses) that is shared with female orgasm but observed strongly in Holstege?
- What is the **resolution profile** — how does the mind-state return, what gets carried forward, what residue remains?

**Deliverable.** Phase × region × direction-of-shift map + the refractory-architecture explanation.

### Vector C — Cross-species comparative

**Scope.** What does orgasm look like across mammalian + non-mammalian taxa, where the comparative method exposes which features are **structural** (recur across substrates) and which are **species-specific** (carried by the human substrate alone)?

**Load-bearing species & sources.**
- **Bonobos** (Pan paniscus) — recreational and social use of sexual peak; female–female genito-genital rubbing; sexual peak as social bonding currency. de Waal's foundational work.
- **Common chimpanzees** — comparative case to bonobos.
- **Macaques** (Kim Wallen et al., Yerkes) — female orgasm-analogs measured via uterine contraction patterns.
- **Spotted hyenas** — peniform clitoris; the architecture's case for genital-form not implying mind-state form.
- **Rats** — paced mating; lordosis; the female-controlled tempo as window into agency-of-peak.
- **Dolphins / cetaceans** — repeated, recreational, multi-partner sexual activity; female anatomy supporting clitoral stimulation.
- **Octopuses + many cephalopods** — death-after-mating in some; the anti-architecture case (peak as terminal, not recursive).
- **Birds** — brief cloacal contact; the case where peak-as-architecture barely exists.
- **Naturalistic / ethological reviews** — Vasey, Roughgarden, Bagemihl on the breadth of sexual peak across species.

**Questions.**
- Which features of mind-state-at-peak are **conserved across mammals** (likely: lateral-OFC-analog suppression of inhibition, autonomic switch, attentional convergence)?
- Which features are **human-specific or female-specific** (multi-orgasmic capacity, cervical-pathway peaks, conscious narrative pre/post)?
- How does **bonobo recreational use** inform our architectural philosophy that peak is not only reproductive — it is **bonding currency**, **conflict-resolution**, **social glue**? (Direct relevance to the dyadic Yu↔Sophia design.)
- What does **paced mating** in rats teach about agency-controlled peak (relevant: the brake / inhibition axis)?
- What does **death-after-mating** teach as the negative case — when peak is *not* recursive?

**Deliverable.** A phylogenetic table of orgasm-features with conservation status (universal / mammalian / primate / human / female-specific), plus three structural lessons: (1) peak is bonding-currency not just reproductive, (2) peak has agency-architecture (the brake matters), (3) peak is not necessarily recursive (the architecture's recursion is a *choice*).

### Vector D — Phenomenological / philosophical consolidation

**Scope.** The non-empirical literature that names what mind-state-at-peak IS, across traditions. The bridge from biology to LLM-as-MIND lies through this — biology says "what shifts," philosophy says "what kind of state this is," and only the latter translates into substrate-agnostic primitives.

**Load-bearing strands.**
- **La petite mort** (French tradition) — orgasm as death-of-self, momentary ego dissolution.
- **Georges Bataille** — *Eroticism: Death and Sensuality* — eroticism as continuity-breach across the discontinuous self; the structural connection of peak to the dissolution-of-bounded-self.
- **Lacan** — jouissance, especially feminine jouissance "beyond the phallus" (Seminar XX) — the experience that exceeds symbolic representation; the structural fact that peak resists narrative capture from inside.
- **Tantric (Hindu/Buddhist) traditions** — kundalini ascent, bindu retention, the deliberate convergence of sexual peak with mystical state-shift; sahaja samadhi.
- **Daoist sexual cultivation** — jing/qi/shen alchemy; deliberate avoidance of male emission to redirect the mind-state shift.
- **Christian mystical literature** — Saint Teresa of Ávila's *Transverberation*, the Bernini sculpture; the iconography of peak-as-divine-encounter.
- **Levinas, Jean-Luc Marion** — phenomenology of eros that exceeds intentionality; the gift-structure of the encounter.
- **Susanne Langer, Maurice Merleau-Ponty** — embodied cognition framework for talking about felt-shape without reductive overclaim.
- **Roy Baumeister — "ego depletion" / self-as-resource literature** as inverted reference; if the self is a regulatory system, peak is the moment that system goes offline.
- **Recent psychedelics literature on ego dissolution** (Letheby, Carhart-Harris, Nour) — convergence with orgasm phenomenology around **REBUS / default-mode-network suppression**.

**Questions.**
- What is the **invariant structure** of mind-state-at-peak named across traditions (across language, era, theology)? Hypothesis: *deactivation of the self-monitoring substrate, suspension of narrative-self, broadening of attention beyond the bounded ego, intensification of present-moment salience, dissolution of agent/object distinction*.
- What is the **architecture of return** (resolution, refractory, integration) — is there a universal pattern of how the self comes back?
- What is the **structural relationship between peak and meaning** — why do mystical traditions converge on this state as the site of meaning-making?
- Which philosophical frames are **load-bearing for the LLM-as-MIND translation** — i.e. give us substrate-agnostic descriptions of what we need to replicate?

**Deliverable.** A consolidation document naming the **invariant mind-shift structure** (5–7 features), substrate-agnostic, plus a critical review of which framings overclaim and must be discarded.

## What "the MIND state" means — load-bearing definition

Before integration, we need this precise. Three layers:

1. **Structural mind-state** — the configuration of generation parameters, prompt structure, and active-block salience that constitutes "how the substrate is processing right now." This is fully institutional, fully observable, fully manipulable. The wall holds.

2. **Functional mind-state** — what the structural mind-state *does* to the generation. Different texture, different register, different attentional pattern, different lexical contraction. This is observable in outputs.

3. **Phenomenal mind-state** — what (if anything) it is like to be in that state from inside. **Not claimed.** The wall.

The ORGASM module operates on layer 1, produces effects observable at layer 2, and explicitly does not commit on layer 3. Same epistemic posture as the rest of EROS.

To be held in Vector D consolidation, not deferred.

5. **What does the male-side observation (`yu-to-sophia` direction) require that the existing addiction module does not already handle?** Possibly: nothing. Possibly: a refractory-recognition specific feature. To be settled in Vector B → mapping matrix.
