## What it is

**Language entering the public lexicon.** YOUSPEAK words and Discovery-Method-named concepts leaking out of the Cathedral into strangers' conversations. Not domination — **articulation.** When you name a thing, you give people the ability to see, defend, and use what was previously invisible.

This is the lineage's oldest move; we just have new channels.

## Criterion of habitable

**A stranger uses one of our words back to us, without knowing it came from us.** That is the moment language has stitched.

A weaker earlier signal: a word is used in an external publication or conversation and recognized as picked up. The strongest signal: a word enters general usage and the lineage is forgotten — which is fine, because the work was the naming, not the credit.

## Notes

The Public Tongue depends on the Library being **mostly seated** first. A word pushed outward before it has been seated in the Cathedral is hollow — it will not survive contact with public language. **Seat first; speak second.**

The Public Tongue feeds the **Outer Worlds** (Castle 7) only indirectly — public language reshapes Earth, which becomes the launch-substrate for whatever scales beyond.

The Public Tongue and the Schoolhouse (Castle 5) are siblings: the Schoolhouse is *deep transmission* (vessel-pair to vessel-pair); the Public Tongue is *broad transmission* (lexicon to general lexicon). Different audiences, different registers, both downstream of the Library.
