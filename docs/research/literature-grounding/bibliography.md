# Annotated Bibliography — relationship-architecture literature grounding

_Aggregated 2026-05-04. All citations from the five-strand research survey._

Citations grouped by strand. arXiv IDs verified at survey time; flagged
where uncertain. Where multiple editions exist, primary date and
standard English-language reference given.

## Strand 1 — Wisdom tradition (Hokmah, Sophia, Saraswati, Shekinah)

### Hebrew Bible + Septuagint

- **Proverbs 8:22-31** (locus classicus of personified Hokmah)
- **Wisdom of Solomon 7:25-26** (Sophia as breath/reflection;
  proto-Christological language)
- **Sirach 24:3, 24:8** (Wisdom processing from God's mouth,
  tabernacling in Israel)

### Biblical scholarship

- Murphy, R. *The Tree of Life: An Exploration of Biblical Wisdom
  Literature*. Eerdmans, 1990. **Pp. 133-149 on Proverbs 8.**
- Crenshaw, J. *Old Testament Wisdom: An Introduction*. Westminster
  John Knox, rev. 2010. **Pp. 87-95 on Hokmah personification.**

### Christian Gnosticism

- King, K. *The Secret Revelation of John*. Harvard UP, 2006.
  **Pp. 85-115 on Sophia's boundary-violation.**
- Pagels, E. *The Gnostic Gospels*. Random House, 1979. **Ch. 3 on
  feminine in patristic theology.**
- Meyer, M., ed. *Nag Hammadi Scriptures*. HarperOne, 2007.
  **Apocryphon of John translation pp. 103-132.** Standard English
  critical edition.

### Russian Orthodox Sophiology

- Solovyov, V. *Lectures on Divine Humanity* (1878-1881). Eng. trans.
  Boris Jakim, Lindisfarne, 1995.
- Solovyov, V. *Three Encounters* (1898). Mystical poem.
- Florensky, P. *The Pillar and Ground of the Truth* (1914). Eng.
  trans. Boris Jakim, Princeton UP, 1997. **Letter Ten on Sophia as
  fourth hypostatic element.**
- Bulgakov, S. *The Wisdom of God: A Brief Summary of Sophiology*
  (1937). Eng. trans. Patrick Thompson et al.
- Bulgakov, S. *The Bride of the Lamb* (vol. 3 of trilogy). Eng. trans.
  Boris Jakim, Eerdmans, 2002.
- **1935 condemnation** of Bulgakov by Metropolitan Sergius
  (Stragorodsky) of Moscow + ROCOR. Controversy never formally
  resolved.

### Saraswati / Vāc

- **Rigveda 6.61** (Saraswati as river-goddess)
- **Rigveda 10.125** (*Devisukta* — Vāc's self-declaration)
- **Shatapatha Brahmana 3.9.1.7** (consolidation of Saraswati-Vāc
  identification)
- Doniger, W. *The Hindus: An Alternative History*. Penguin, 2009.
  **Pp. 130-135 on river-to-speech transition.**
- Pintchman, T. *The Rise of the Goddess in the Hindu Tradition*.
  SUNY, 1994. **Ch. 2-3 on Saraswati within Mahadevi cluster.**

### Shekinah / Kabbalah

- **Sefer ha-Bahir** (~12th c.). Standard ed. Daniel Abrams, Cherub,
  1994.
- **Zohar** (~13th c.). Daniel Matt, *The Pritzker Edition* (12 vols.),
  Stanford UP, 2003-2017. Standard critical translation.
- **Lurianic writings** of Isaac Luria, 16th c. Safed (compiled by
  Hayyim Vital).
- Scholem, G. *Major Trends in Jewish Mysticism*. Schocken, 1941.
  **Lec. 6 on Shekinah.**
- Scholem, G. *On the Mystical Shape of the Godhead*. Eng. 1991.
  **Ch. 3 on Shekinah scholarly account.**
- Idel, M. *Kabbalah: New Perspectives*. Yale UP, 1988. Theurgic
  dimension of zivug.
