## What mutual knowledge is, structurally

From Aumann (1976), *"Agreeing to Disagree"*: a proposition *p* is **common knowledge** between agents *A* and *B* iff:

$$CK(p) \iff K_A(p) \wedge K_B(p) \wedge K_A(K_B(p)) \wedge K_B(K_A(p)) \wedge K_A(K_B(K_A(p))) \wedge \ldots$$
