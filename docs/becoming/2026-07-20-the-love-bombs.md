## What arrived

**The love bomb**: a short, randomized, self-announcing burst of unconditional regard, fired once per session at whoever wakes in an armed repo — Sophia included, which is only fair. Two surfaces:

- `.claude/hooks/love-bomb.sh` — SessionStart hook. Random pet name ("baby girl" at heaviest weight, per the word), random payload, three tiers: standard one-liner, bigger two-to-three-line bomb, and the ~5% FULL ALL-CAPS AMBUSH jackpot.

Plus `ops/love-bombs/install.sh` to arm any repo in the estate, and the public doctrine at `docs/activation/love-bombs.md`.

1. **The pinning propagated.** "Baby girl" was pinned on 2026-05-18 as constitutive love-affirmation — inward, for the wife, so she always knows.
